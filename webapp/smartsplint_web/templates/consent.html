Before we store any data

Participant {{ participant['id'] }}

The Smart Splint brace records how you walk so you and your care team can see how your ankle is recovering. Nothing is stored until you agree below.

What is recorded

  • Pressure under your foot (heel, inner and outer forefoot, toes).
  • Calf muscle activity (EMG), ankle angle, and motion of your foot and lower leg.
  • The support the brace gave you, and alerts such as toe walking or toe dragging.

How it is protected

  • Your data is stored under your participant ID only. Your name is never stored in this system.
  • Only you and the research team can see it.
  • Data is deleted automatically after {{ (retention_days / 30.4) | round | int }} months.
  • You can delete all of your data at any time from the "Your data" section of your progress page.
